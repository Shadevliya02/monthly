Guide & definitions

What each page and number means, in plain words.

How to use this website

  1. Dashboard gives the summary. Pick a period at the top right: 2026 for the whole year, or a single month.
  2. Tiles with a red calendar tag (for example “Sep 2026”) follow that period. Not paid yet and Waiting longest count invoices submitted from January up to that month (tag “Jan–Sep 2026”), with their status as of today. The monthly chart always shows the whole year.
  3. Hover or tap the ⓘ next to a tile title to read what it measures.
  4. Click a month bar, a partner, a status or a View link to open the matching invoice list.
  5. Invoices lists every invoice. Use the filters to narrow it down, then click a row to see its full timeline.
  6. Process flow shows the whole payment process step by step, from BAPB / BAI to Finance, and who does each step.

The payment process at a glance

  1. 1BAPB / BAI · goods arrived or installed
  2. 2Request & BAST number
  3. 3Signing the BAST
  4. 4Invoicing · tracked here from the submit date
  5. 5Payment Voucher (PV)
  6. 6Submit to Finance → paid · tracked here as the paid date

Terms

Outbound
Invoices from partners for outbound work: Cross Connect and Colocation.
Inbound
Invoices for inbound work. In the table, Type Project shows “Project Delta”.
Cross Connect
A cable link (fiber optic or UTP) that connects a carrier’s or ISP’s equipment directly to a customer’s equipment inside the data center.
Colocation
Space in the data center rented for a customer’s equipment: a shared rack, a dedicated cage, or a whole private room.
Working Room
An office room inside the data center that a customer can use, dedicated or shared. Often appears in invoice descriptions.
BAST value
The invoice amount, taken from the BAST.
Submit date
The day the invoice was submitted to TDE. “Invoices received” counts by this date.
Paid date
The day the invoice was paid. “Total paid” counts by this date.
Days to get paid
Paid date minus submit date, in calendar days.
Paid
Payment is done.
In review
Submitted and not paid yet. Documents are being checked or processed.
Returned
Sent back to the partner to fix. Needs follow-up.
Not paid yet
In review plus Returned. On the Dashboard, counted by the month the invoice was submitted.
Days waiting
For invoices not paid yet: today minus submit date.
Return / Resubmit
Each time an invoice is sent back to the partner (return) and sent again (resubmit). Numbered 1, 2, 3 when it happens more than once.
Return aging
Where the time went for a returned invoice: Review (TDE checking, before each return), Partner (fixing, from return to resubmit) and Payment (from the last resubmit to paid).
vs last month
Change compared with the month before. Green means better, red means worse, grey is neutral. Shown only when a month is selected.
